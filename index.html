How ordering works

Three steps. No app, no sign-up.

  1. 1

    Pick your food

    Tap Add on any dish. Your order is saved in this browser.

  2. 2

    Add your details

    Your name, mobile number, and Pickup or Delivery. We ask for an address only for Delivery.

  3. 3

    Send on WhatsApp
