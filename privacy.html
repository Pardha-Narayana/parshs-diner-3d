Privacy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What we collect

When you send an order, we ask for your name, your mobile number, and Pickup or Delivery. We ask for your address only if you choose Delivery. Notes are optional. We also keep the dishes you chose, the total and an order number.

Why

Only to prepare your order, and to deliver it if you chose Delivery.

Where it goes

Your order goes to two places:

  1. The restaurant’s WhatsApp (+91 77805 30607), as a message you send yourself. WhatsApp is run by Meta.
  2. The restaurant’s own Google Sheet, our order list. Google Sheets is run by Google.

What stays on your device

Your cart and your last order are saved only in your own browser, so you can reorder later. We save just the dishes and the order number there, never your name, phone or address. Clearing your browser data removes them.

What we don’t do

No tracking. No analytics. No ads. We never sell or share your details.

The company that hosts this website may keep basic technical records, like your IP address, to keep the site running.

Ask us to delete your order

We delete order records from our order list after 6 months.

Send a WhatsApp message to +91 77805 30607 (opens WhatsApp in a new tab) with your order number (like PD-1432) or your phone number. We will delete it from our order list. This can’t remove the message from your own WhatsApp.
